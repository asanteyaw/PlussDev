PlussAI

The machine-learning layer of Pluss: classic for classical ML, nets for neural modules, nlp for text workflows, lymph for high-level modeling, ice for training/runtime orchestration, and procedure for artifact-driven execution.

API Reference

What's in this space

PlussAI's public surface is organized by family, with human-facing namespaces layered over the internal implementation spaces.

classic Classical ML Core

The public classical-ML namespace over the internal NodeJ substrate: batches, splits, prep, metrics, families, tuning, and persistence contracts.

nets Neural Modules

The neural/module surface over the internal NodeK implementation: modules, losses, embeddings, RNNs, attention, convolution, and optimization helpers.

nlp Text & Language

The public NLP namespace over the internal NodeB layer: docs, tokenization, vectorization, embeddings, text runtimes, and NLP workflows.

lymph Model Workspace

High-level modeling, validation, selection, spotchecking, workflow composition, and convenience wrappers over the classical ML core.

ice Training & Runtime

Training loops, sources, coaches, outlines, model orchestration, and deep-learning convenience surfaces over the neural family.

procedure Execution & Persistence

Artifact-driven staged execution, family-aware persistence, and reusable execution graphs across PlussAI components.
