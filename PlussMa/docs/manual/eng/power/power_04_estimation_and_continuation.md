# Power 4: Estimation and Continuation

Two questions complete the analysis of a power system. What are the voltages, when all that is known is a set of noisy measurements? And how far can the load grow before the voltage collapses? This chapter estimates the state of a network from redundant, weighted measurements and detects bad data, and follows the voltages through increasing load past the point where the power flow fails.
