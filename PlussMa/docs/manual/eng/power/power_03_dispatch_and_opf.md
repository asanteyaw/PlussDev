# Power 3: Dispatch and Optimal Power Flow

A system operator must meet the demand every hour with the generators it has, at least cost, without overloading a line and, ideally, without risking a blackout after the next line trips. This chapter chooses the generation: by economic dispatch in the linear DC model, with and without security against the loss of any line, and by the exact AC optimal power flow with losses, reactive power and voltage limits.
