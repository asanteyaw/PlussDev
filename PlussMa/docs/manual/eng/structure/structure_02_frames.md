# Structures 2: Frames

A frame is made of members that are joined rigidly and so resist bending as well as stretching. Where a truss needs pin joints and triangles, a frame can be a beam, a column, a portal or a grid of them. This chapter describes a frame to the solver, solves beams against the classical formulas, shows how distributed loads and member end actions are handled, and solves a portal frame whose sway stiffness depends on how stiff its beam is.
