# Structures 3: Dynamics

Real structures move. A frame vibrates at its own natural frequencies, a column buckles under a compressive load far below its strength, a floor responds to a footfall, and a machine on a beam shakes it at its running speed. This chapter finds the natural frequencies and mode shapes of a frame, its buckling loads, its response in time to loads that change and its steady response to a sinusoidal load at each frequency, always with beams whose answers are known in closed form.
