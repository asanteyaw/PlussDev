# Robotics 3: Constraints and Contacts

A robot touches things: a foot on the floor, a tip pinned to a wall, a gripper on a rail. This chapter holds a robot to such constraints. A pin keeps a point from accelerating, a contact keeps a point on one side of a plane and can only push, friction opposes sliding, an impact law stops the arrival of a point at a plane, and in every case the forces and impulses come out of the dynamics together with the accelerations.
