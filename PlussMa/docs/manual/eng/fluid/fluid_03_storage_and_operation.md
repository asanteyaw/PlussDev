# Fluid 3: Storage and Operation

A water system is operated, not just built. Demand follows the hour, tanks fill and empty, a customer receives less water when the pressure falls, and pumps and valves are switched by the clock and by the water level. This chapter chooses how demands behave when the pressure is low, and then runs the network through time: tanks, demand patterns, and controls that act on a clock or on a level.
