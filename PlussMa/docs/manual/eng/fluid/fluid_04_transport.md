# Fluid 4: Transport

Knowing the flows is half the question about a distribution system. The other half is what the water carries: a disinfectant that decays on its way, a contaminant that spreads from one source, the age of the water in a dead end, the heat of a district heating main. This chapter follows a constituent and the age of the water through the flows of a run, and then the temperature with its exchange with the ground.
