# Biology

Biological models describe how quantities such as population sizes, concentrations and forces change in time. The guides begin with populations: how a single species grows and how its growth is limited.

1. **How does a population grow?** Exponential, logistic and Gompertz growth, with closed forms to check a numerical integration against and batches of growth rates solved together.

2. **How do species affect each other?** Predators and prey, and communities of competing species.

3. **What is the future of a population?** Stage matrices, lifetimes, sensitivities and the chance of extinction.

4. **What can a metabolic network do?** Flux balance, flux variability and knockouts.
5. **How fast does an enzyme work?** Rate laws with inhibitors, and progress curves.

6. **How far does an epidemic go?** The SIR model, its closed-form outcome and contact rates.
7. **How does a drug move through the body?** Compartment models, exposure and repeated dosing.

8. **How do genes change in a population?** Selection and drift.
9. **How does a neuron fire?** The leaky neuron and firing patterns.
10. **What does a sequence say about a protein?** Charge and pH.

11. **How does a muscle produce force?** Force curves, and the balance between fibers and tendon.

12. **How does diffusion create patterns?** Turing instability.
13. **How does blood flow?** Resistance, pulsation, wave speed and the Windkessel.

14. **How do genes control each other?** Switches and clocks made of repressors.
15. **How do ligands bind?** Depletion, competition and cooperativity.

16. **How does a membrane hold and change a voltage?** Balance voltages, the resting voltage and the action potential.
17. **How stable is a protein?** Unfolding by denaturant and by temperature.
18. **How do soft tissues deform?** Hyperelastic stress and stretch.
19. **How do molecules and populations spread?** Diffusion and invasion fronts.

20. **Which enzyme controls a pathway?** Elasticities and control coefficients.
21. **How does a delay change a population?** Delayed growth and its stability.
22. **How do populations structured by size behave?** Integral projection models.
23. **How do we read extinction risk from counts?** State-space fitting of census data.
24. **How does DNA melt?** Nearest-neighbour thermodynamics.

25. **How do reaction networks behave?** Rates, steady states and conservation laws.
26. **Where does evolution of a trait lead?** Singular strategies and branching.
27. **How do you fit enzyme progress curves?** Integrated fits with intervals, product inhibition and prediction.
28. **How do limbs of several links move?** Statics, forward dynamics and control of a linked chain.
29. **When is a community stable, and who can invade?** Random communities, May's threshold and assembly.
30. **What is the reproduction number of a structured epidemic?** The next-generation method for stages, strains and vectors.
31. **Which genes are essential?** Gene knockouts on a flux-balance model.
32. **What fluxes can a network support?** Elementary modes and flux sampling.
33. **How do you rule out thermodynamically impossible loops?** Loopless flux analysis.
34. **How does control work with conserved moieties?** Control coefficients when totals are conserved.
35. **Is a pathway thermodynamically feasible?** Transformed energies and driving forces.
36. **When do population maps cycle or turn chaotic?** Period detection, Lyapunov exponents and periodic points.
37. **How do parasitoids and hosts coexist?** Nicholson-Bailey maps and clumped attack.
38. **How do patches and harvest shape a metapopulation?** Occupancy, capacity and yield.
39. **How does a nerve impulse travel?** Hodgkin-Huxley conduction, speed against radius and cables.
40. **How do you simulate reaction-diffusion?** Boundary conditions, front speed and Turing patterns.
41. **How do random environments change growth?** Stochastic growth rates and quasi-extinction.
42. **How does pH change binding?** Proton linkage and apparent constants.
43. **How do enzyme mechanisms differ?** Two-substrate mechanisms and allosteric enzymes.
