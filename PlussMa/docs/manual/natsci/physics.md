# Physics

Physical models describe motion and flow with equations that are solved on many cases at once. The guides begin with two systems that have exact solutions to check against: a body orbiting another under gravity, and an incompressible flow in a periodic box.

1. **How does a body orbit?** Orbital elements, period, exact propagation and conserved energy.
2. **How does a fluid flow?** Incompressible flow, the pressure projection and convergence against an exact solution.
3. **How does a quantum system evolve?** A driven two-level atom and its decay.
4. **How do gases exchange heat and work?** The ideal gas and the paths of a process.
5. **How do mechanical systems move?** Hamilton's and Lagrange's equations and symplectic integration.
6. **How do gases move at high speed?** Shock tubes, wall and open boundaries, and viscous gas with its step limits.
7. **How do you simulate turbulence you cannot resolve?** Large-eddy closures: Smagorinsky, WALE and the dynamic model.
8. **How do you model turbulence you only average?** Reynolds-averaged closures in a channel, checked against the law of the wall.
9. **Where is a body on its orbit at a given time?** Kepler's equation for elliptic and hyperbolic orbits.
10. **How do you get from one orbit to another?** Lambert transfers between two positions in a given time.
11. **How do many bodies move under gravity?** Gravitating systems, conserved quantities and the figure-eight orbit.
12. **What are the thermodynamic properties of a species?** Polynomial fits for heat capacity, enthalpy and entropy.
13. **What are a quantum system's energy levels, and what does noise do to it?** Spectra, ground states and quantum channels.
