# Physics

Physical models describe motion and flow with equations that are solved on many cases at once. The guides begin with two systems that have exact solutions to check against: a body orbiting another under gravity, and an incompressible flow in a periodic box.

1. **How does a body orbit?** Orbital elements, period, exact propagation and conserved energy.
2. **How does a fluid flow?** Incompressible flow, the pressure projection and convergence against an exact solution.
3. **How does a quantum system evolve?** A driven two-level atom and its decay.
4. **How do gases exchange heat and work?** The ideal gas and the paths of a process.
5. **How do mechanical systems move?** Hamilton's and Lagrange's equations and symplectic integration.
