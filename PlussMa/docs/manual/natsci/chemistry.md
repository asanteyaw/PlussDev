# Chemistry

Chemical models describe how molecules bind, react and assemble. The guides begin with three topics that have clean closed forms to check against: gases on surfaces, polymer chains, and crystals forming from solution.

1. **How do gases stick to a surface?** Adsorption isotherms, competition between gases and the surface area of a powder.
2. **What is in a polymer sample?** Chain lengths, molar-mass averages, step-growth and copolymers.
3. **How do crystals form?** Nucleation barriers and the Avrami growth curve.
4. **How fast is a reaction?** Transition-state theory, activation parameters and tunnelling.
5. **How do cells and electrodes behave?** Nernst potentials, electrode kinetics and ionic activities.
6. **What is in a solution at equilibrium?** Aqueous speciation and complexation.
7. **How does light measure a sample?** Absorption, line shapes and diatomic thermodynamics.
8. **How do gases carry heat, momentum and matter?** Transport properties of pure gases and mixtures.
9. **How does a gas mixture react and equilibrate?** Mechanisms, equilibrium composition and reactors, including ignition.
10. **How do we compute electrons in a molecule?** Restricted and unrestricted Hartree-Fock.
11. **How do polymer chains move?** Rouse and Zimm dynamics.
