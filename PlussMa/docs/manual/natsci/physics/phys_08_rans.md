# Physics 8: Reynolds-averaged turbulence

A Reynolds-averaged simulation solves for the mean flow and models all of the turbulence as an eddy viscosity from an extra transport equation. These pages solve the turbulent channel with two models, check it against the law of the wall and the exact stress balance, and couple the models to the incompressible flow solver.

- [The turbulent channel](phys_08_rans/turbulent_channel.md)
- [Coupling a model to the flow solver](phys_08_rans/coupled_model.md)
