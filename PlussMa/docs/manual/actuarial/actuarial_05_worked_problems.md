# Actuarial 5: Worked Problems

The earlier chapters build the tools one at a time. This chapter uses them together on five complete problems, each posed as a practitioner would meet it, solved step by step with programs and their output, checked by a second route, and then read: what the numbers say, what they depend on, and what to carry forward. Each problem uses the operators and functions of earlier chapters and has the same shape: the problem, the plan with its mathematics, the steps, the results read in prose, and a short list of points to keep.

- [Pricing a Term Life Policy](actuarial_05_worked_problems/pricing_a_term_life_policy.md): Price a ten-year term policy: net and gross premium, a cashflow check, the reserve and the sensitivity to mortality and interest.
- [A Deferred Retirement Annuity](actuarial_05_worked_problems/a_deferred_retirement_annuity.md): Price an annuity that starts at 65: the single premium as a pure endowment times an annuity, instalments, a refund of premiums, retirement age and longevity.
- [Designing a Reinsurance Programme](actuarial_05_worked_problems/designing_a_reinsurance_programme.md): Aggregate losses, the cost and price of three excess-of-loss layers, a simulation check and the effect of a non-Poisson claim count.
- [A Rate Review by Territory](actuarial_05_worked_problems/a_rate_review_by_territory.md): Experience, credibility and loads to an indicated rate change, then a cap on the change and the revenue it costs.
- [A Reserving Review](actuarial_05_worked_problems/a_reserving_review.md): Stability of the development pattern, chain ladder, a tail factor, Bornhuetter-Ferguson as a credibility mix, and sensitivity.
