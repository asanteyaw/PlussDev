# Actuarial 2: Life Contingencies, Premiums and Reserves

Mortality turns into money when a contingent payment is discounted and weighted by the probability that it is paid. This chapter values the basic benefits of a life, shows the operator that values any benefit, lays out the classical commutation columns, and builds level premiums and reserves from them.
